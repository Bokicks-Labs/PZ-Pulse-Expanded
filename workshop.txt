title=PZ Pulse Expanded
description=PZ PULSE, WITH MORE OF YOUR CHARACTER STATE
description=
description=PZ Pulse Expanded is a Build 42 add-on for PZ Pulse that replaces its Moodles & Needs+ panel with a mod-aware Needs Expanded view.
description=
description=If its source mod is not enabled, its data has not synchronized, or its value cannot be read safely, that individual row simply does not appear. The rest of the panel continues to work.
description=
description=REQUIRED MOD
description=PZ Pulse: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3753700423
description=Load PZ Pulse Expanded after PZ Pulse. Optional supported mods may load before PZ Pulse Expanded. Fully restart Project Zomboid after changing the mod list.
description=
description=NEEDS EXPANDED
description=- PZ Pulse's existing vanilla Needs+ bars
description=- Active vanilla moodles
description=- Vanilla Discomfort stat
description=- Active moodles discovered from supported installed mods
description=- Optional continuous meters from supported mods
description=- Source-aware tooltips
description=- Every stat bar uses the corresponding vanilla or source-mod moodle icon
description=
description=Inactive moodles stay hidden. Stats with no safe value are hidden independently, so one missing integration never empties or breaks the whole panel.
description=The moodle area matches PZ Pulse's original design: original glyphs on colored moodle circles with the current level in the bottom-right. The only layout change is less space between the moodles and status bars.
description=
description=OPTIONAL MOD INTEGRATIONS
description=No mod in this section is required.
description=
description=- Lifestyle: Hobbies: discovers active entries from Lifestyle's LSMoodles registry and adds a nullable Hygiene Need meter.
description=- Take A Bath And Shower: supports its Moodle Framework states and adds a nullable Body Grime meter.
description=- Nature's Call: supports its active bladder, bowel, soilage, smell, gut, and related moodles, plus independently nullable Bladder and Bowel meters.
description=- Moodle Framework: displays active registered moodles from installed add-ons, including unknown registrations under a generic source label.
description=- DrugTime 89: supports its active Stoned and Hard states.
description=- Medicine Moodles: supports its registered medicine-effect moodles.
description=- Brush Your Damn Teeth: supports its mouth-state moodle and a nullable Dental Hygiene meter.
description=- Infirmities: supports panic attacks, tremors, addictions, and withdrawal.
description=- The Mutants: supports its Puked moodle.
description=
description=Integrations are read-only. PZ Pulse Expanded does not create, initialize, or mutate another mod's player data just to display it.
description=

description=COMPATIBILITY AND MULTIPLAYER
description=- Made for Project Zomboid Build 42.20+.
description=- PZ Pulse is the only declared dependency.
description=- Optional integrations activate only when their mod is active and readable.
description=- In multiplayer, the client can display only state that the source mod makes available locally or synchronizes to that player.
description=- Clients and servers should use matching mod lists and versions where a server-side source mod is involved.
description=
description=CURRENT SCOPE
description=This first release focuses on Needs Expanded. Skills Expanded and the public third-party provider API are planned work and are not advertised as complete features in this release.
description=
description=Back up important saves before changing any mod list. If a supported mod changes its private data format, that integration is designed to disappear safely rather than stop the rest of PZ Pulse from rendering.
