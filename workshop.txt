title=PZ Pulse Expanded
description=[h1]ONE DASHBOARD. MORE OF YOUR CHARACTER.[/h1]
description=
description=See your survivor's needs, hidden vanilla stats, and active modded moodles together in Needs Expanded — a replacement for PZ Pulse's Moodles & Needs+ panel, built for Project Zomboid Build 42.
description=
description=[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3753700423]PZ Pulse[/url].[/b] Load PZ Pulse Expanded after PZ Pulse, then restart the game after changing your mod list. Supported integrations are optional.
description=
description=[h2]SEE MORE AT A GLANCE[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Keep PZ Pulse's familiar Needs+ bars alongside active vanilla and supported mod moodles.
description=[*]Track vanilla Discomfort and other useful player-state stats that aren't shown in the original panel.
description=[*]Add optional meters such as Bladder, Bowel, Hygiene Need, Body Grime, Dental Hygiene, addictions, drug intensity, and Caffeine Level when their source data is available.
description=[*]Read what a stat means and where it comes from with source-aware tooltips.
description=[*]Match bar colors to active moodle levels where available.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]MAKE THE PANEL YOURS[/h2]
description=Use the panel's gear menu to manage stat bars: show or hide individual bars or whole sources, pin bars, and reorder them. Tune moodle and stat icon sizes independently. Optional auto-hide keeps idle bars out of the way; a reveal flash can call attention when one returns.
description=
description=Inactive moodles stay hidden. A stat with unavailable or unreadable source data is omitted on its own, so an optional integration won't break the rest of the panel. Saved choices for inactive sources remain available if you enable them later.
description=
description=[h2]OPTIONAL MOD SUPPORT[/h2]
description=No integrations below are required. When the corresponding mod is active and its data is readable, Needs Expanded can show:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Lifestyle: Hobbies[/b] — active registered moodles and Hygiene Need.
description=[*][b]Take A Bath And Shower[/b] — bathing, body-temperature, and related moodles; Body Grime.
description=[*][b]Nature's Call[/b] — bladder, bowel, soilage, smell, and related moodles; Bladder and Bowel meters.
description=[*][b]Infirmities[/b] — panic, tremors, addiction, and withdrawal moodles; addiction meters when enabled and readable.
description=[*][b]DrugTime 89[/b] — Stoned and Hard moodles with intensity meters.
description=[*][b]Brush Your Damn Teeth[/b] — mouth-state moodle and Dental Hygiene.
description=[*][b]Caffeine Makes Sense[/b] — Caffeine Level.
description=[*][b]Moodle Framework add-ons[/b] — active registered moodles, including supported medicine effects and unrecognized registrations.
description=[*][b]The Mutants[/b] — Puked moodle.
description=[/list]
description=Integrations are read-only: this add-on observes existing source-mod data and does not create or initialize it.
description=
description=[h2]COMPATIBILITY[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Made for Project Zomboid Build [b]42.20+[/b].
description=[*][b]PZ Pulse is the only required mod.[/b]
description=[*]In multiplayer, only state available locally or synchronized by the source mod can be shown. Match client and server mod lists and versions when a server-side source mod is involved.
description=[*]If an optional source changes or its data is unavailable, its affected rows are omitted independently.
description=[/list]
description=
