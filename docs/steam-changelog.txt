[h1]PZ Pulse Expanded - Changelog[/h1]

[h2]Version 1.3.0 - More Stat Bars and Panel Controls[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Added 12 continuous bars[/b] from DrugTime 89, Infirmities, Caffeine Makes Sense, and supported vanilla stats, including Nicotine Withdrawal and Poison.
[*]Added Manage Stat Bars to the Needs Expanded gear menu. Show or hide bars individually or by source, pin bars, and change their display order. Choices persist in this browser; unavailable mod controls are disabled without losing saved choices.
[*]Added optional auto-hide for idle bars, a configurable hide delay, and an optional white outline flash when a hidden bar first appears.
[*]Colored bars to match active moodle levels where available, with source-based thresholds for directly related stats and a severity scale for bars without a matching moodle.
[*]Expanded stat and moodle tooltips with gameplay effects, restored their hover delay, and made them stable across panel updates.
[*]Adjusted moodle and stat icon sizes and spacing, added a Caffeine Level coffee icon, and gave Poison its own icon.
[*]Added independent moodle and bar icon size controls to Needs Expanded; the original Needs+ size settings no longer change this panel.
[*]Bar icon size changes in quarter steps and bar thickness grows with the icons. Auto-hide now defaults to a 10-second delay; reveal flash is enabled by default for 7 seconds when auto-hide is on.
[*]Slowed the reveal flash and let its final blink fade out fully instead of stopping mid-pulse.
[*]Rounded Lua-collected stat values to two decimal places.
[/list]

[h2]Version 1.2.1 - Stable Continuous Stat Bars[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Fixed Nature's Call Bladder and Bowel bars disappearing during multiplayer snapshot gaps.[/b]
[*]After the first server snapshot, valid local Nature's Call values keep the bars updated while the owner snapshot is temporarily absent.
[*]Discomfort and supported optional mod stat bars now retain their last valid values through brief failed reads. Persistently missing values still disappear.
[*]Added a Missing stat grace period setting under Options > Mods > PZ Pulse Expanded. Default: 90 seconds; set it to 0 to disable retention.
[*]Disabled mods and intentionally hidden stats still disappear immediately. Moodle behavior is unchanged.
[/list]

[h2]Version 1.2.0 - Stat Bar Icons[/h2]

[list]

[h2]Version 1.1.0 - PZ Pulse-Style Moodles and Expanded Mod Support[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Redesigned the Needs Expanded moodle area to match the original PZ Pulse panel.[/b]
[*]Active moodles now appear as their original glyph on PZ Pulse's colored moodle circle, with the current level shown in the bottom-right corner.
[*]Reduced the padding between the moodle area and the status bars for a more compact layout.
[*]Added or expanded active-moodle support for Lifestyle: Hobbies, Take A Bath And Shower, Moodle Framework, Nature's Call, DrugTime 89, Medicine Moodles, Brush Your Damn Teeth, Infirmities, and The Mutants.
[*]Added source-aware handling for recognized mod moodles while retaining generic Moodle Framework support for unknown registrations.
[*]Kept every optional moodle and stat independently nullable. Missing mods, unsynchronized data, or unreadable values no longer prevent the rest of the panel from rendering.
[*]Kept all integrations read-only: PZ Pulse Expanded does not create, initialize, or mutate another mod's player data to display it.
[*]Added an automated artwork build step so the Workshop package embeds the committed source-mod moodle glyphs consistently.
[/list]

[h2]Third-Party Moodle Artwork Added in 1.1.0[/h2]

To reproduce supported moodles faithfully, version 1.1.0 added only the moodle glyph artwork required by these integrations:

[list]
[*][b]Lifestyle: Hobbies[/b] - all 63 PNG moodle glyphs from its common/media/ui/moodles folder.
[*][b]Take A Bath And Shower[/b] - BodyGrime, CoolingBody, FeelingGaze, WarmingBody, and Wet_Bathing.
[*][b]Infirmities[/b] - its nine Panic, Tremors, Addiction, and Withdrawal moodle glyphs.
[*][b]Medicine Moodles[/b] - Antibiotics, Antidepressants, BetaBlockers, LemonGrass, Painkillers, and SleepingTablets.
[*][b]Brush Your Damn Teeth[/b] - BYDT_mouth_state.
[*][b]The Mutants[/b] - PZTheMutants_Puked.
[*][b]DrugTime 89[/b] - all six DT_Hard and DT_Stoned level glyphs.
[*][b]Nature's Call[/b] - CheckPants, PissBladder, StoolIcon, SoiledClothing, both Gut Health states, Stomach Cramp, and Acid Reflux.
[/list]

PZ Pulse's vanilla glyphs and colored moodle circles are reused from the required [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3753700423]PZ Pulse[/url] mod at runtime and are not copied into PZ Pulse Expanded.

[b]All third-party artwork remains the property of its respective creator. No ownership is claimed. If a mod author or rights holder asks for their artwork or integration to be removed, contact me and I will remove it promptly.[/b]
