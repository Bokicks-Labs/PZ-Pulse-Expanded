PZ Pulse Expanded - Changelog

Version 1.5.0 - Bodily Functions Support

- Added optional Bodily Functions Bladder and Bowel bars, using configured capacities and the source mod moodle levels for bar colors.
- Added the five active Bodily Functions moodles with their original glyphs and source attribution.
- Kept Nature's Call and Bodily Functions readings separate; PZ Pulse Expanded does not resolve conflicts between their gameplay systems.
- Updated the Workshop description with Bodily Functions artwork credit and a warning that supported mods may conflict with one another.

Version 1.4.1 - Drug Euphoria Auto-Hide Fix

- Fixed Drug Euphoria remaining visible at zero when auto-hide is enabled and the bar is not pinned.

Version 1.4.0 - More Optional Mod Support and Stat Health

- Added three Lifestyle: Hobbies continuous meters: Embarrassment, Comfort, and Beauty.
- Added Casualties Undead support for its Drug Euphoria meter, High and Miserable moodles, and the worst active clinical condition when player state is available.
- Added familiar Casualties Undead glyphs and distinct Brush Your Damn Teeth icons for Dental Hygiene and the short-term Mouth State moodle. Dental Hygiene follows the mod's hygiene-effect thresholds.
- Simplified Lifestyle: Hobbies meter labels to Hygiene, Comfort, and Beauty, and refreshed the Workshop description and screenshot guidance for optional-mod support and community feedback.
- Added per-bar collection health to Manage Stat Bars, including waiting placeholders, current or recently retained readings, and clear unavailable or problem states.
- Added validation for PZ Pulse Needs+ rows and recovery from adapter read failures, with bounded diagnostics.
- Improved stat bar source-state reporting and fixed valid zero values for Casualties Undead euphoria.

Version 1.3.0 - More Stat Bars and Panel Controls

- Added 12 continuous bars from DrugTime 89, Infirmities, Caffeine Makes Sense, and supported vanilla stats, including Nicotine Withdrawal and Poison.
- Added Manage Stat Bars to the Needs Expanded gear menu. Show or hide bars individually or by source, pin bars, and change their display order. Choices persist in this browser; unavailable mod controls are disabled without losing saved choices.
- Added optional auto-hide for idle bars, a configurable hide delay, and an optional white outline flash when a hidden bar first appears.
- Colored bars to match active moodle levels where available, with source-based thresholds for directly related stats and a severity scale for bars without a matching moodle.
- Set four-level negative bars to green-yellow, yellow, orange, and PZ Pulse red; three-level bars use green-yellow, yellow, and red. Positive levels progress through greens. Moodle badges are unchanged.
- Expanded stat and moodle tooltips with gameplay effects, restored their hover delay, and made them stable across panel updates.
- Adjusted moodle and stat icon sizes and spacing, added a Caffeine Level coffee icon, and gave Poison its own icon.
- Added independent moodle and bar icon size controls to Needs Expanded; the original Needs+ size settings no longer change this panel.
- Bar icon size changes in quarter steps and bar thickness grows with the icons. Auto-hide now defaults to a 10-second delay; reveal flash is enabled by default for 7 seconds when auto-hide is on.
- Slowed the reveal flash and let its final blink fade out fully instead of stopping mid-pulse.
- Rounded Lua-collected stat values to two decimal places.

Version 1.2.2 - Lifestyle Hygiene Ownership

- Hide the Lifestyle hygiene stat bar when an active CW Modpack Compatibility mod disables Lifestyle's hygiene need.
- Keep Take A Bath And Shower body grime and Brush Your Damn Teeth dental hygiene bars independent of that setting.
- Fix optional stat collection in the Project Zomboid Lua runtime so supported mod bars no longer disappear with a repeated console error.

Version 1.2.1 - Stable Continuous Stat Bars

- Fixed Nature's Call Bladder and Bowel bars disappearing during multiplayer snapshot gaps.
- After the first server snapshot, valid local Nature's Call values keep the bars updated while the owner snapshot is temporarily absent.
- Discomfort and supported optional mod stat bars now retain their last valid values through brief failed reads. Persistently missing values still disappear.
- Added a Missing stat grace period setting under Options > Mods > PZ Pulse Expanded. Default: 90 seconds; set it to 0 to disable retention.
- Disabled mods and intentionally hidden stats still disappear immediately. Moodle behavior is unchanged.

Version 1.2.0 - Stat Bar Icons

- Added a corresponding moodle icon to every built-in stat bar.
- Vanilla Needs+ stats and Discomfort now use their matching PZ Pulse moodle icons.
- Nature's Call Bladder and Bowel use their original source-mod icons.
- Lifestyle: Hobbies Hygiene Need, Take A Bath And Shower Body Grime, and Brush Your Damn Teeth Dental Hygiene use their corresponding source-mod icons.
- Preserved nullable behavior: if a source mod is inactive or a value or icon cannot be read safely, that individual item is omitted or degrades safely without breaking the panel.
- Added automated coverage for all 18 currently supported built-in stat rows.

Version 1.1.0 - PZ Pulse-Style Moodles and Expanded Mod Support

- Redesigned the Needs Expanded moodle area to match the original PZ Pulse panel.
- Active moodles now appear as their original glyph on PZ Pulse's colored moodle circle, with the current level shown in the bottom-right corner.
- Reduced the padding between the moodle area and the status bars for a more compact layout.
- Added or expanded active-moodle support for Lifestyle: Hobbies, Take A Bath And Shower, Moodle Framework, Nature's Call, DrugTime 89, Medicine Moodles, Brush Your Damn Teeth, Infirmities, and The Mutants.
- Added source-aware handling for recognized mod moodles while retaining generic Moodle Framework support for unknown registrations.
- Kept every optional moodle and stat independently nullable. Missing mods, unsynchronized data, or unreadable values no longer prevent the rest of the panel from rendering.
- Kept all integrations read-only: PZ Pulse Expanded does not create, initialize, or mutate another mod's player data to display it.
- Added an automated artwork build step so the Workshop package embeds the committed source-mod moodle glyphs consistently.

Third-Party Moodle Artwork Added in 1.1.0

To reproduce supported moodles faithfully, version 1.1.0 added only the moodle glyph artwork required by these integrations:

- Lifestyle: Hobbies - all 63 PNG moodle glyphs from its common/media/ui/moodles folder.
- Take A Bath And Shower - BodyGrime, CoolingBody, FeelingGaze, WarmingBody, and Wet_Bathing.
- Infirmities - its nine Panic, Tremors, Addiction, and Withdrawal moodle glyphs.
- Medicine Moodles - Antibiotics, Antidepressants, BetaBlockers, LemonGrass, Painkillers, and SleepingTablets.
- Brush Your Damn Teeth - BYDT_mouth_state.
- The Mutants - PZTheMutants_Puked.
- DrugTime 89 - all six DT_Hard and DT_Stoned level glyphs.
- Nature's Call - CheckPants, PissBladder, StoolIcon, SoiledClothing, both Gut Health states, Stomach Cramp, and Acid Reflux.

PZ Pulse's vanilla glyphs and colored moodle circles are reused from the required PZ Pulse (https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3753700423) mod at runtime and are not copied into PZ Pulse Expanded.

All third-party artwork remains the property of its respective creator. No ownership is claimed. If a mod author or rights holder asks for their artwork or integration to be removed, contact me and I will remove it promptly.
